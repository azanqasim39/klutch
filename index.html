The Story of Sialkot Sweets & Bakers

75 Years of Pure Sweet Heritage
In the Heart of Gujranwala

Founded by Ishaq & Sons

Being founded by a family passionate about traditional confectionary, Sialkot Sweets and Bakers started its journey in the 1950s; promising to bring forth a variety of sweets and bakery rich in quality, and flavour. From our humble beginnings as a local shop in Sialkot, we have grown into a household name; celebrated for bringing flavours of Pakistani sweets to local, as well as the international market.

Over the years, we haven't only preserved our authentic recipes passed down by generations, but have successfully expanded our immaculate sweet offerings.

Today, we proudly present recognised sweets like Rasgulla, Guldasta, Gulab Jamun, Barfi and Halwayat etc, along with our bakery items, like fresh cake, cookies, cake rusk and khatai etc., all made with high quality ingredients and upheld hygienic standards.

We warmly welcome you all to our ever-growing family!
